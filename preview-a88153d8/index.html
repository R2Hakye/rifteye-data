How it works

Three steps, about a minute.

Add RiftEye to Chrome

From the Chrome Web Store, then pin it to your toolbar.

Open a Riftbound stream

On Twitch or YouTube. RiftEye starts by itself and outlines the cards within seconds.

Point at a card

See it full size. Busy layout? Press Alt+Shift+B and box the table.

Privacy

Your screen stays yours.

On your computer

Cards are recognised inside your browser, on your graphics card when it can. Video frames never leave your computer.

Nothing collected

No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. Your settings stay in your browser.

Only what it needs

RiftEye runs on Twitch and YouTube only, and downloads just card pictures and new card data.

Open about it

Every detail is in the privacy policy.
