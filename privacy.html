RiftEye Privacy Policy

Effective 15 October 2026

RiftEye is a browser extension that names the Riftbound cards shown on Twitch and YouTube streams and shows a tooltip with the card when you point at it. This page explains what it reads, what it keeps and what it sends.

In short

  • Card recognition runs entirely inside your browser. The video you watch is never uploaded anywhere.
  • RiftEye has no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. It collects no personal data.
  • It downloads only card pictures from Riot Games' image server and, when a new set comes out, updated card data from GitHub.
  • Your settings stay on your computer. Removing the extension deletes them.

What RiftEye reads

While RiftEye is switched on, the tab is visible and you have marked the table area of a stream, it takes still pictures of that area of the video, about once a second. It also reads a small region when you ask it to name a particular card. These pictures go to the recognition engine, which is part of the extension and runs on your computer. They are processed in memory and then discarded. They are never saved and never sent off your computer.

RiftEye runs only on twitch.tv and youtube.com. It does not read the chat, your Twitch or YouTube account, your history or any other site.

What RiftEye sends over the internet

Card pictures for the tooltips are downloaded from Riot Games' image server, cmsassets.rgpub.io. As with any picture a website loads, Riot's server receives your IP address and ordinary browser information with each request. The request names the card picture only. It does not say which stream you are watching. Riot's own privacy notice covers that server. RiftEye keeps the pictures in memory and does not save them.

A few times a day RiftEye checks GitHub (github.com, which serves the files from release-assets.githubusercontent.com) for updated card data, and downloads it when a new set comes out. GitHub receives your IP address and ordinary browser information, as with any download. The request is the same for everyone and says nothing about you or what you watch. GitHub's privacy statement covers that server.

Nothing else is sent. There are no analytics, crash reports or usage statistics.

Development builds only (not the version in the Chrome Web Store): if you change “Card engine” to “Local server”, the pictures described above go to a program on your own computer (localhost) instead of the built-in engine. They still never leave your computer.

What RiftEye keeps on your computer

WhatWhereHow long
Your settings (on/off, tooltip options, which engine to use)Browser extension storage, on this computerUntil you change them or remove the extension
The table area you marked, for each Twitch channel or YouTube video, labelled with the channel name or video IDBrowser extension storage, on this computerUntil you remove the extension
Updated card data downloaded from GitHubBrowser extension cache, on this computerUntil a newer set replaces it or you remove the extension
Decklists you paste and the cards named so farMemory onlyUntil the browser closes

None of this is synchronised to your Google account or sent to us. To erase all of it, remove the extension.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell, rent or share personal data. We have none to share.
  • We do not show ads or use data for advertising, profiling or credit decisions.
  • We do not load or run code from the internet. All of RiftEye's code ships inside the extension; the card data it downloads is data only, checked against published checksums.

RiftEye's use of information complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Permissions, and why

  • Access to twitch.tv and youtube.com: to show the overlay and read the video's table area.
  • Access to cmsassets.rgpub.io: to download card pictures for the tooltips.
  • Access to RiftEye's data on github.com and githubusercontent.com: to download updated card data.
  • Storage: to remember your settings and table areas on this computer.
  • Offscreen document: to run the recognition engine in the background, once for all tabs.

Children

RiftEye does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If RiftEye ever starts sending or keeping anything new, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
